7. Provide concrete comparison evidence for every reported part.
8. Do not guess an eye part merely because eyes are visually salient. Report it only when reference/expected-state comparison supports it.
9. A swap must name both parts in the pair.
10. A correct assembly has zero affected detected_parts.
